About Legal Saathi

Know the law. Know your rights.

Legal Saathi is an educational project designed to make basic Indian legal information easier to understand in Hindi and English.

It is not a law firm, does not create an advocate-client relationship and cannot replace advice from a qualified lawyer.

For free legal aid, contact the relevant Legal Services Authority. Visit NALSA.

Important disclaimer: Laws and procedures can change. This website provides general educational information, not legal advice. Verify current law from official sources and consult a qualified advocate for advice about a specific matter. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
